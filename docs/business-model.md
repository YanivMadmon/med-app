# Business Model — MedApp

## Who Pays?
The **caregiver (adult child)** pays — not the elderly user.
The elderly user's interface is always free and simple.
This is key: the person with pain (worry about their parent) pays for peace of mind.

---

## Subscription Plans

| Plan | Price | Included |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | $0/month | Up to 3 medications, 1 caregiver |
| **Pro** | $8/month | Unlimited medications, up to 5 caregivers, weekly reports, PDF export |
| **Family** | $15/month | Up to 3 patients, unlimited caregivers, all Pro features |

## Why This Pricing Works
- Medisafe (market leader) moved to paid in Jan 2026 — created a gap
- $8/month is less than one coffee per week for peace of mind about a parent
- Family plan targets adult siblings sharing care responsibilities

## Go-To-Market Strategy

### Phase 1 — Israel First
- Hebrew-first product = immediate differentiation
- Target Facebook groups for adult children caring for parents
- Partner with Israeli pharmacies and HMOs (Kupat Holim)

### Phase 2 — English Markets
- Expand to US, UK, Australia
- App Store + Google Play optimization (ASO)
- Content marketing: "how to manage elderly parent medications"

### Phase 3 — B2B
- Sell to nursing homes and assisted living facilities
- White-label for health insurance companies
- API for integration with HMO systems
